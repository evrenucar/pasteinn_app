> Use case: logo-brand
> Asset type: version 2 transparent greeting lettering for a cozy app landing page.

> Text (verbatim): "{phrase}"

> Composition/framing: exact phrase on a wide single line filling most of the canvas, with small safe transparent margins that keep every letter and punctuation fully visible.
> Constraints: genuinely transparent background with alpha. Only the lettering and exact punctuation. No fine cursive, no delicate calligraphy, no connected script, no conventional rigid arcade font, no thin strokes. No texture particles, no ornamental extras, no shadow, no glow, no gradient, no background, no watermark. Bold coarse-square pixel strokes are the central requirement.

The founder rejected both the original glowing gradient and the later excessive/uniform grain. The app icon is a small original code-native SVG; category library icons are Lucide, with its bundled license. Custom user drawings are data, not generated brand artwork.
