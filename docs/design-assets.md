## Pixel handwriting

File: `dist/assets/a-little-space.png` (2170 × 725, transparent PNG).

Current use: large visual heading in the app preview and landing page. This is an original raster lettering study, not a complete font. A full typeface or editable lettering system remains future design work.

Exact prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand
> Asset type: transparent wide editorial lettering image for an app.
> Input images: Image 1 is a style reference ONLY for the irregular handwritten stepped-pixel character of its lettering. Do not reproduce its wording, background, arrangement, exclamation mark or other shapes.
> Primary request: render exactly "a little space." as warm cream irregular hand-drawn pixel handwritten letters.
> Text (verbatim): "a little space."
> Style/medium: oversized loose human handwritten strokes formed of stepped square pixels, relaxed imperfect baseline, minimalist beautiful editorial mark. Thin-to-medium naturally inconsistent strokes, charming human asymmetry and open forms like the reference. NOT a conventional arcade, monospace or block font.
> Composition/framing: all lettering on one wide line, generous framing so every letter and the final period is fully visible.
> Color palette: warm cream lettering.
> Constraints: genuinely transparent background with alpha; only the exact lowercase lettering and its period; no other shapes, no illustration, no background, no shadow, no watermark.
